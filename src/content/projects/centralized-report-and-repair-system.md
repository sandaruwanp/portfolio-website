## Overview

Centralised Repair and Report System is a platform designed to simplify the process of reporting, tracking, and managing repair requests. It connects users with responsible teams through a single system for efficient issue resolution.

## Problem

Traditional repair reporting methods are often slow, unorganized, and lack proper tracking. Users face difficulties in submitting complaints, while authorities struggle to manage and prioritize requests effectively.

## Approach

The system provides a centralized platform where users can submit repair reports, monitor progress, and receive updates. It improves communication, transparency, and workflow management between users and repair teams.

## Lessons

The project highlighted the importance of user-friendly design, efficient data management, and clear communication workflows. It also provided experience in developing solutions that address real-world service management challenges.
